
### **Week 1: Foundations**

#### **Day 1: Understand the Basics**
- **LLD**:
    - Learn Object-Oriented Principles (OOP): Encapsulation, Inheritance, Polymorphism, and Abstraction.
    - Review SOLID Principles: Single Responsibility, Open-Closed, Liskov Substitution, Interface Segregation, Dependency Inversion.
    - Study Design Patterns: Singleton, Factory, Observer, Strategy.
- **HLD**:
    - Understand architecture basics: monolithic vs. microservices, layered architecture, and client-server models.
    - Learn about common architectural patterns: MVC, MVVM, and event-driven architecture.

#### **Day 2-3: Practice LLD Concepts**
- Practice designing classes and interfaces for common problems:
    - Library Management System
    - Parking Lot
    - ATM Machine
- Focus on:
    - Class diagrams
    - Proper usage of OOP principles
    - Relationships like aggregation, composition, and inheritance

#### **Day 4-5: Deep Dive into HLD Components**
- Study fundamental system design components:
    - Load Balancers
    - Databases (SQL vs. NoSQL)
    - Caching (Redis, Memcached)
    - Message Queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ)
- Learn about **CAP Theorem** and trade-offs in distributed systems.

#### **Day 6-7: Mock Problems**
- **LLD Mock Problems**:
    - Design a Snake and Ladder game.
    - Design an E-commerce Shopping Cart.
- **HLD Mock Problems**:
    - Design URL Shortener (TinyURL).
    - Design a Chat System like WhatsApp.

---

### **Week 2: Advanced Topics**

#### **Day 8-10: Explore Advanced Design Patterns**
- LLD:
    - Behavioral Patterns: Command, Chain of Responsibility, Observer.
    - Structural Patterns: Adapter, Composite, Proxy.
- HLD:
    - Architectural Patterns: Event Sourcing, CQRS, Saga.

#### **Day 11-12: Learn About Scalability**
- Study concepts like:
    - Vertical vs. Horizontal Scaling.
    - Partitioning and Sharding.
    - CDN (Content Delivery Network).

#### **Day 13-14: Practice Problem Solving**
- **LLD**:
    - Design a Movie Ticket Booking System.
    - Design a Food Delivery App.
- **HLD**:
    - Design an Online Video Streaming Service (e.g., Netflix).
    - Design a Social Media Feed.

---

### **Week 3: Hands-On System Design**

#### **Day 15-16: Focus on Database Design**
- Learn to design normalized schemas for:
    - E-commerce platforms.
    - Ride-sharing applications.
- Understand the pros/cons of denormalization in HLD.

#### **Day 17-18: Work on Scalability and Availability**
- Analyze how to handle:
    - High traffic.
    - Data consistency (eventual consistency vs. strong consistency).
- Practice designing systems for large-scale use cases.

#### **Day 19-21: Practice Mock Interviews**
- Conduct mock interviews focusing on:
    - Clear communication.
    - Drawing diagrams and explaining trade-offs.
- Example Problems:
    - **LLD**: Design a Notification System.
    - **HLD**: Design a Cloud Storage Service like Google Drive.

---

### **Week 4: Review and Mock Interviews**

#### **Day 22-24: Full Mock Interviews**
- Spend time solving both LLD and HLD problems under interview-like conditions.
- Practice whiteboarding or digital diagram tools (e.g., Excalidraw, Miro).

#### **Day 25-26: Identify Weaknesses**
- Review past problems where you struggled.
- Focus on improving:
    - LLD: Proper class decomposition and relationships.
    - HLD: Better scalability and fault tolerance mechanisms.

#### **Day 27-28: Final Preparation**
- Revise key concepts, patterns, and trade-offs.
- Ensure you can confidently:
    - Draw and explain a system diagram.
    - Justify design choices based on requirements.

---

### **Key Tips During Interview:**
1. **Clarify Requirements**: Understand functional and non-functional requirements thoroughly.
2. **Think Aloud**: Explain your thought process clearly.
3. **Iterate Gradually**:
    - Start with a high-level overview.
    - Drill down into specifics based on interviewer questions.
4. **Trade-offs**: Always discuss trade-offs and alternatives.
